# Running a trial

The numbers in [results.md](results.md) come from simulation and load tests. A trial at a real
place (a clinic, a salon, a placement drive, a service desk) gives numbers from real people.
This is how to run one and report it honestly.

## Before the day

1. **Create a line** on the home page with the place's name, its counters (rooms, desks, chairs,
   panels) and a rough number of minutes per person. The estimate learns from there.
2. **Print the poster** ("Print a poster" on the staff screen). Put one at the entrance and one at
   the reception desk.
3. **Set up the staff screens.** Open the staff link on whatever the front desk uses, a phone or a
   laptop. "Copy the staff link" shares it with the other counters; each person picks their
   counter once.
4. **Optional: the TV.** "Open the TV screen" on a laptop or smart TV in the waiting area, then
   "Turn on sound" so it chimes on every call.

## On the day

- Press **Call next** when you're ready for the next person. If nobody comes, **Call again**, then
  **Didn't come**. If they turn up later, **Back to the front**.
- Someone without a smartphone: **Add someone without a phone** gives them a number to note
  down. The TV shows it when it's their turn.
- **Pause joining** stops new people from joining near closing time without turning away those
  already waiting; **Close** at the end of the day. Reopening an empty line restarts the numbers
  at 1 and keeps the totals.

## Afterwards

- The staff screen's **Since the line was created** card has the totals for the whole trial:
  - how many people were served;
  - how often the wait shown when someone joined turned out right (their real wait fell inside the
    range, give or take a minute);
  - how far off the estimate was on average.
- **Download the report** for one row per ticket: when they joined, were called and finished,
  how long they waited, what they were shown, and whether it was right. It has no names.
  Finished tickets are deleted after 7 days, so download it within a week.
- Report what you measured, with its context: the kind of place, the days, how many people, how
  many counters, and how many of them used their own phone. Simulated demo customers are never
  counted.

## What to tell the place

- Their customers need no app and no account; names are optional and only staff see them.
- Nothing is sold or shared. Finished tickets, with any names on them, are deleted after 7 days,
  and a line nobody uses for 30 days is deleted entirely.
- It's free, and their paper tokens still work alongside it if they want a fallback.
